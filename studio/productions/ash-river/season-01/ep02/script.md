# ASH RIVER

## Season 1, Episode 2 — "THE CLINIC"

*Running time: 7:00. Beat ids in the right margin correspond to `beatsheet.yaml`.*

---

### 0:00 — SCENE 1: THE ANSWER — INT. BUS, MOVING `b01`

Straight off the freeze. No recap. The same second.

Maren has the mic in her hand. She keys it.

> **MAREN**
> Dispatch nine, say again your unit.

Carrier. One second. Two.

> **COYLE (RADIO)**
> You're going to want to be past the Coldwater junction before dusk, Ash River bus.
> The wind turns at dusk.

He did not answer the question. She notices. She does not ask it again.

She puts the mic back in the cradle and drives.

---

### 0:10 `b02`

Under her breath, at the mirror:

> **MAREN**
> Nine. Ten. Eleven.

She stops. She already knows what the next number is.

JOSIE arrives at her shoulder, holding the chart against her chest.

> **JOSIE**
> Why did we slow down?
>
> **MAREN**
> Grade's steeper than it looks on that side.

It is. That is not why they slowed down.

Josie accepts it. She goes back down the aisle counting, out loud, from one.

---

### 0:38 — SCENE 2: COLDWATER ROAD — INT./EXT. `b03`

Ash comes down on the windscreen.

Not much. Steady. Like the beginning of snow, if snow were grey and arriving on the
wrong side of the glass.

The wipers move it and it comes back.

> **COYLE (RADIO)**
> Junction's eleven miles. You've got about forty minutes of margin. Use it.

---

### 1:06 `b04`

*No dialogue.*

Maren gets out of the seat.

She walks the aisle — the first time she has left the wheel — and counts by hand, one
palm on each seat back, because she does not trust the mirror any more.

At the back: the emergency door. The latch is unseated. Not open. Unseated.

She reseats it. She looks at it for one second longer than a person looks at a latch.

She goes back to the wheel.

---

### 1:36 — SCENE 3: THE FREQUENCIES — INT. BUS `b05`

Sam's footage has been round the whole bus and come back as an argument.

> **GUS**
> It's a fire. Fires do what they want.
>
> **SAM**
> Six of them. In a row. In a *row*, man —
>
> **GUS**
> Turn it off.

Quietly, from the window seat:

> **INES**
> Eleven hundred feet.

Everyone looks at her.

> **DELL**
> What?
>
> **INES**
> The spacing. It's eleven hundred feet.

She does not say anything else. She goes back to the window.

---

### 2:04 `b06`

GUS takes the bus set. He is going to prove Ines wrong, and he is entitled to.

He walks the county bands. One. Dead air. Two. Dead air. Three, four, five.

Nothing on any of them. Not static — nothing.

He gets to the one that has Coyle on it.

He listens to it for a moment.

He puts the handset back in the cradle, does not say anything to anybody, and sits down.

---

### 2:30 — SCENE 4: THE FLAG — EXT. COLDWATER JUNCTION `b07`

They make the junction eight minutes early. Nobody celebrates, because NEL has seen the
sign.

**COLDWATER CLINIC — 2**

> **NEL**
> Two miles.
>
> **MAREN**
> No.
>
> **NEL**
> Brianne's got until about six tomorrow morning on what's in that bag. You know that
> better than I do, because you loaded it.

Behind them, at the corner of frame, a mailbox flag stands straight out toward the bus.

Nobody looks at it.

---

### 2:58 `b08`

The clinic. Dark. Locked.

The evacuation notice is taped on the inside of the glass, and it is dated four days
before the fire.

Nel reads it. Her face does something small. She does not say a word about it.

> **NEL**
> Side door.
>
> **MAREN**
> Ninety seconds.
>
> **NEL**
> Ninety seconds.

She goes.

---

### 3:28 — SCENE 5: NINETY SECONDS — EXT. CLINIC `b09`

Maren counts from the step of the bus, out loud, and it is the only sound in the episode.

> **MAREN**
> Twenty. Twenty-five. Thirty.

INES is not watching the clinic. She is watching the ditch.

> **INES**
> Not over the culvert.
>
> **MAREN**
> What?
>
> **INES**
> North side floods first. Don't park over it.

Maren moves the bus four feet.

She does not ask how Ines knows. Nobody does.

---

### 3:56 `b10`

> **MAREN**
> Seventy. Seventy-five.

From inside the bus, to nobody in particular:

> **BRI**
> It's got warmer.

> **DANNY**
> *(not turning round)*
> That's the fire, love.

Beat.

> **BRI**
> It's got warmer.

Nobody hears her the second time either.

She is the only person on this bus breathing bottled air. She is the only one whose
sense of the outside is uncontaminated. She is right.

---

### 4:22 — SCENE 6: NEL — EXT./INT. `b11`

*No dialogue.*

> **MAREN (V.O.)**
> A hundred and ten.

She pulls out.

She takes the Coldwater turn without looking at the sign.

In the mirror: the clinic side door, standing open, and nobody in it.

---

### 4:50 `b12`

*No dialogue.*

Twenty-two seconds.

THEO turns around in his seat and watches the road behind them, long after there is
anything back there to watch.

JOSIE takes out her chart. She holds it. She does not open it.

---

### 5:12 — SCENE 7: NINE MILES — INT. BUS `b13`

> **COYLE (RADIO)**
> Good work on the junction. You bought yourself twenty minutes there.

Ash on the windscreen. Still coming down. Still landing on the glass in front of her.

Maren watches it for four seconds.

Then she winds the window down and puts her hand out flat, the way you do, and holds it.

The wind is on the *back* of her hand.

It has been on the back of her hand since the clinic. Ash falls downwind. It has been
landing in front of them for nine miles because they have been driving into it for nine
miles.

The wind did not turn at dusk.

The wind turned an hour ago, and the man who told them when to expect it has been
counting those nine miles with them.

She winds the window up. She says nothing on the radio at all — and there is no ident
tone under his carrier. There never has been.

---

### 5:48 `b14`

*No dialogue.*

She turns the bus.

Forty feet of school bus in a farm gateway on a road built for a pickup, in four
movements, with the front left wheel out over the culvert Ines told her about.

Nobody helps her. Nobody could.

It is the last place on this road wide enough to do it.

---

### 6:18 — SCENE 8: THE EMPTY SEAT — INT. BUS `b15`

GUS comes forward. He crouches in the aisle so it is only for her.

> **GUS**
> Ask him his badge number.

Maren has the mic in her hand. She looks at it.

She puts it back in the cradle without keying it.

> **GUS**
> Maren.
>
> **MAREN**
> Sit down, Gus.

---

### 6:44 `b16`

*No dialogue.*

On the way back to the wheel she counts one more time. By hand. Seat back by seat back.

Eleven.

She stops.

She counts again, the other way, from the front.

Eleven.

She puts her palm flat on the twelfth seat.

It is empty.

It is warm.

Both doors have been shut since Coldwater.

**FREEZE** on her hand on the seat.

**CUT TO BLACK.**
