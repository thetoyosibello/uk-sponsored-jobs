# ASH RIVER — Season 1 Arc

12 episodes × 7 minutes. Written backwards from the L5 twist in E12.

Fixed points, placed before anything else: **L4 at E08** (~66% of season), **L5 at E12**,
**L3 at E03 / E06 / E09 / E12**. Everything else was written around them.

Every episode has a stated **goal** and a **real goal**. The gap between them is the L2
twist. Twelve episodes, twelve buttons, twelve questions — no episode ends on a
resolution.

---

## Tracks

**Resource ladder.** Fuel (exhausts E04) → the route (exhausts E08) → Maren's hands and
conscience (exhausts E12). Bri's oxygen runs underneath as a second visible gauge.

**Boundary.** Miles of playable road, per episode:
`41 → 38 → 33 → 29 → 24 → 19 → 15 → 11 → 8 → 5 → 2 → 0.4`
It only goes down. When it reaches zero they are walking.

**Attrition.** First loss E02 to establish the rules, then a hold, then acceleration.
`E02: Nel · E05: Gus · E07: Dell · E10: Danny · E12: one more, and Maren chooses which.`

## E01 — "Headcount"

- **Goal:** get eleven people out of Ash River before the fire crosses Ridge Road.
- **Real goal (L2):** there are twelve people on a bus that boarded eleven.
- **Cost:** the cabin stops being safe. She has to drive with her back to all of them.
- **Loops opened:** the road out · her hands · why she counts from nine · the boy ·
  the voice on the radio · the new gate · the fire's straight line · the twelfth passenger.
- **Boundary:** 41 miles. **Options: 6 → 1.**
- **Button:** Coyle asks for a headcount. She says "Eleven." He says, *"That's right.
  Eleven."* — **why did she lie, and how did he know?**

## E02 — "The Clinic"

- **Goal:** reach the Coldwater turn before the wind shifts at dusk.
- **Real goal (L2):** the wind already shifted. They have been driving into it for nine miles.
- **Cost:** Nel goes back for the clinic and does not come back. The rules are now established.
- **Button:** the twelfth passenger's seat is empty, and the door was never opened.

## E03 — "Dispatch 9" · **L3-A**

- **Goal:** raise county and confirm the route.
- **Real goal:** county went down forty minutes before the bus left. Gus's handheld has
  been dead all day, and nobody asked why the bus radio isn't.
- **L3-A:** there is no Dispatch 9. She is on a private channel.
- **Cost:** the only navigation they have is the only thing they cannot trust.
- **Button:** Maren keys the mic anyway.

## E04 — "Siphon"

- **Goal:** get fuel out of the Prentice yard tanks.
- **Real goal (L2):** the tanks are full and the fuel is contaminated — someone drained
  the clean stock three weeks ago.
- **Cost:** tier-1 resource exhausted. From here the bus is running on what it has.
- **Button:** Theo has drawn the gate. He drew it before they reached it.

## E05 — "Procedure"

- **Goal:** hold the bus at the Carrow junction until an escort arrives.
- **Real goal (L2):** the escort is what Coyle needs them to wait for. Nothing is coming.
- **Cost:** Gus, following procedure into the one thing procedure did not anticipate.
- **Button:** Ruth says Maren's old call sign out loud, and the cabin goes quiet.

## E06 — "What Ines Mapped" · **L3-B**

- **Goal:** find a way onto the fire road above the burn.
- **Real goal (L2):** the fire road is where they are being steered.
- **L3-B:** Ines surveyed the ignition line. She filed the warning. It was buried. She
  is not a saboteur — she is the only other person on the bus who thinks this is her fault.
- **Cost:** the audience's distrust was aimed at the wrong person for six episodes.
- **Button:** Ines asks Maren what happened at Coldwater. Maren answers.

## E07 — "Nine"

- **Goal:** get Dell's arm treated before he loses it.
- **Real goal (L2):** Dell has known about the twelfth passenger since E01 and has been
  waiting for someone to ask him.
- **Cost:** Dell. He is right the third time, and it costs him.
- **Button:** she counts, and gets ten.

## E08 — "The Line" · **L4 — the season twist**

- **Goal:** reach the north gate and cut the chain.
- **Real goal:** the gate was installed three weeks before the fire. The ignition points
  form a line, not a spread. **The fire was set, and the evacuation was routed by the
  office that set it.** Every earlier obstacle converts from bad luck to intent.
- **Cost:** tier-2 exhausted — there was never an open road. And the reframing lands on
  Maren hardest: she has been driving to someone else's plan since the first minute.
- **Button:** thirty seconds after the reveal, the wind turns. New threat, immediately —
  a reframe left to breathe deflates into exposition.

## E09 — "The Notebook" · **L3-C**

- **Goal:** get Theo across the wash on foot.
- **Real goal (L2):** Theo is not lost. He is placed.
- **L3-C:** the notebook carries Maren's old crew call sign, in a nine-year-old's hand.
  He did not wander onto this bus.
- **Cost:** the protective duty — the one clean thing in the story — is part of the design.
- **Button:** Theo asks Maren if she remembers his grandfather.

## E10 — "Face"

- **Goal:** reach the repeater and see who is on the other end.
- **Real goal (L2):** he wants to be seen. He has wanted it since the first mile.
- **Cost:** Danny, and the last of the fuel, and the last mile of road.
- **Button:** Coyle, on foot, walking toward the bus. Not running.

## E11 — "Coldwater"

- **Goal:** get the last of them to the gap.
- **Real goal (L2):** the gap is the slot canyon. It has been the destination since E01.
- **Cost:** Maren's hands are gone. She knows what to do and cannot do it — the capability
  gap that has been building for eleven episodes arrives at full size.
- **Button:** Coyle asks her, on the open channel, how many she is bringing out.

## E12 — "Eleven" · **L3-D + L5 — the series twist**

- **Goal:** all of them through the gap.
- **L3-D:** the twelfth passenger is Coyle. He has been aboard since before the door opened.
- **L5:** he is the son of one of the three who did not come back from Coldwater. He has
  not been guiding her out. Every turn took her closer to the same canyon, so she would
  make the same choice in front of witnesses — and **she makes it.**
- **Cost:** everything. The one thing she has protected for eleven years is the belief
  that Coldwater was the wind's fault.
- **Loop ledger:** closes to zero. Every question opened this season is answered or
  explicitly promoted to Season 2.
- **Button:** she starts the count. "Nine." Cut to black on the second number.

## Where the feature cut breaks

Assembled, the twelve episodes run ~84 minutes. Episode buttons become act breaks. The
**L4 lands at 66% of feature runtime**, so the final act plays entirely under the
reframing. The **L5 lands in the last four minutes** and re-reads the whole film — which
is the point of putting it at the top of the ladder.
